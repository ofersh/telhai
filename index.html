Professor of Computer Science · Tel-Hai University, Israel

Teaching algorithms to steer the physical world — from quantum systems to living ones.

We design, analyze and benchmark search and optimization algorithms that act on the physical world: shaping laser pulses that control quantum systems, solving hard combinatorial problems on quantum hardware, and guiding wet laboratory experiments in biology and agriculture.
